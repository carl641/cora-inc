Drain Field (Leach Field) Work in Huntsville, AL

A failing drain field can threaten your entire septic system. Cora Environmental installs, repairs, and rehabilitates leach fields to keep your Huntsville-area system healthy and your yard dry.

What's included

Fix the drain field, save the system

The drain field is where treated water returns to the soil, and when it fails you'll see standing water, soggy patches, and slow drains long before the tank itself is the problem. We diagnose the real cause and install, repair, or rehabilitate the field so the whole system stays healthy.

From new field lines on a Madison County build to correcting a soggy backyard in Owens Cross Roads, we handle the soil work and grading that a lasting repair depends on.

  • New field line installation
  • Repair or replacement of failing drain fields
  • Correction of standing water and soggy yard issues
  • Soil work and proper grading

Local & dependable

Standing water in your yard? Don't wait.

North Alabama's clay-heavy soils are hard on drain fields, and a small problem spreads fast once the field stops absorbing. If you're seeing wet spots or smelling odors in the yard, call us early — catching it sooner often means a repair instead of a full replacement.
