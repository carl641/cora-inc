Commercial Services

Drain & Hydro Jetting in Huntsville, AL

High-pressure water cuts through grease, sludge, and buildup that snakes can't touch. Huntsville commercial kitchens keep this on schedule to keep their drains clear.

What's included

A full reset for clogged commercial drains

Cabling a line punches a hole through a clog; hydro jetting scours the pipe wall clean. Using a focused stream of high-pressure water, we clear grease, scale, and heavy buildup so your drains flow like new — and stay that way longer.

It's the go-to fix for Huntsville-area restaurants fighting slow drains, and we often pair it with a grease trap pump-out so the whole system gets reset at once.

  • Hydro jetting for commercial kitchen drains
  • Clears grease, scale, and heavy buildup
  • Recurring schedules available to prevent blockages
  • Often combined with grease trap pumping
