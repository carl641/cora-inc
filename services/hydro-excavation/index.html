Hydro Excavation (Hydro Vac) in Huntsville, AL

Hydro excavation uses pressurized water and a powerful vacuum to dig precisely without striking utilities or damaging landscaping — a capability that sets Cora Environmental apart in North Alabama.

What's included

The safest way to dig near buried lines

Traditional digging near buried utilities is a gamble. Hydro excavation removes soil with a focused water stream and vacuums it away, so we can expose gas, water, fiber, and sewer lines without ever striking them. It's precise, non-destructive, and clean.

For Huntsville contractors, builders, and property managers, that means daylighting utilities, potholing for locates, and excavating in tight or landscaped areas without the risk of a costly utility strike.

  • Non-destructive digging around buried utilities
  • Daylighting for gas, water, fiber, and sewer lines
  • Potholing and precision excavation
  • Clean, accurate work in tight or landscaped areas
