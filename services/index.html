What We Do Every Day

These are the jobs our trucks roll out on day in and day out.

Septic Tank Pumping

Regular pumping is the single most important thing you can do for your septic system. We remove accumulated sludge and solids to prevent backups, odors, and premature system failure.

  • Residential and commercial pumping
  • Trucks and manpower to clean any kind of system
  • Complete removal of sludge and scum layers
  • Recommended every 3–5 years depending on usage and household size

Learn more about Septic Tank Pumping →

Grease Trap Pumping

Restaurants and commercial kitchens depend on clean traps to stay compliant and keep drains flowing. We pump grease from tanks both above ground and in ground — and we clean the whole tank, not just skim the scum off the top.

  • Above-ground and in-ground grease trap service
  • Complete tank cleaning — not just surface skimming
  • Scheduled routes for restaurants and commercial kitchens
  • Disposal handled to code

Learn more about Grease Trap Pumping →

Drain Jetting & Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water cuts through grease, sludge, and buildup that snakes can't touch. Commercial kitchens keep this on schedule to keep their drains clear — we often pair it with a grease trap pump-out for a full reset.

  • Hydro jetting for commercial kitchen drains
  • Clears grease, scale, and heavy buildup
  • Recurring schedules available to prevent blockages
  • Often combined with grease trap pumping

Learn more about Drain & Hydro Jetting →

Drain Field (Leach Field) Work

A failing drain field can threaten the entire system. We install, repair, and rehabilitate leach fields to keep your system healthy and your yard dry.

  • New field line installation
  • Repair or replacement of failing drain fields
  • Correction of standing water and soggy yard issues
  • Soil work and proper grading

Learn more about Drain Field Work →

Hydro Excavation (Hydro Vac Services)

Hydro excavation uses pressurized water and a powerful vacuum to dig precisely without striking utilities or damaging landscaping. It's the safest way to expose buried lines, daylight utilities, and dig in tight or sensitive spots — and it's a capability that sets us apart in the region.

  • Non-destructive digging around buried utilities
  • Daylighting for gas, water, fiber, and sewer lines
  • Potholing and precision excavation
  • Clean, accurate work in tight or landscaped areas

Learn more about Hydro Excavation →
