Know exactly what you're buying

A septic inspection is one of the smartest things you can do before closing on a North Alabama home — a failing system can be a five-figure surprise. We assess the tank level, baffles, lines, and drain field, then give you a clear picture of the system's real condition.

We handle real estate inspections for Huntsville-area buyers and sellers, plus routine health checks for homeowners who want to stay ahead of problems.
