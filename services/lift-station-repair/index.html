Lift Station & Pump Repair in Huntsville, AL

When a pump fails, wastewater has nowhere to go. Cora Environmental services and repairs lift stations and pump systems across Huntsville to get flow restored fast.

What's included

Get flow restored fast

A failed lift station backs up quickly and doesn't wait for business hours. We troubleshoot the whole system — pump, floats, controls, and alarms — find the fault, and repair or replace it so wastewater keeps moving.

We service both commercial and residential systems throughout Huntsville and North Alabama, and our 24/7 emergency line means help is a phone call away when a station goes down.

  • Lift station troubleshooting and repair
  • Pump replacement and rebuild
  • Float, control, and alarm service
  • Commercial and residential systems
