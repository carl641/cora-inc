Grease Trap Pumping in Huntsville, AL

Restaurants and commercial kitchens across Huntsville depend on clean traps to stay compliant and keep drains flowing. We clean the whole tank — not just skim the scum off the top.

What's included

Grease trap service that keeps you compliant

A grease trap that isn't serviced on schedule overflows into your lines, creates odors, and can put you on the wrong side of a health inspection. We pump grease from tanks both above ground and in ground, then clean the entire tank so it works between visits.

From Huntsville's restaurant row to kitchens in Madison and Hampton Cove, we set up recurring routes so your trap is always handled before it becomes a problem.

  • Above-ground and in-ground grease trap service
  • Complete tank cleaning — not just surface skimming
  • Scheduled routes for restaurants and commercial kitchens
  • Disposal handled to code
