About Us

A Local Team You Can Count On

Cora Environmental is a locally owned and operated septic services company based right here in Huntsville, Alabama. We serve the families and businesses of the Tennessee Valley with honest work and fair pricing.

Our Story

Built on good service and a handshake.

Cora Environmental was founded on a simple idea: septic work should be straightforward, respectful, and done right. After years working for larger outfits, our founders set out to run the kind of company they'd want to hire — one that shows up on time, explains the work in plain English, and treats customers like neighbors (because they usually are).

Today we're proud to serve hundreds of homes and businesses across North Alabama with a full range of septic services, from routine pumping to full system installations.

We also offer contract services for businesses and restaurants to keep their grease pumped and drains jetted on schedule.

Our Values

What we stand for

Honesty First

We won't recommend work you don't need. If your tank doesn't need pumping yet, we'll tell you. If a repair is a better call than a replacement, we'll show you why.

Respect for Your Property

We protect your landscaping, clean up after the job, and leave your yard better than we found it. Heavy equipment shouldn't mean heavy damage.

Do It Right

Every job gets done to code, with proper permits and quality materials. Cutting corners is how you end up doing the same job twice.

Show Up

On-time arrivals, honest ETAs, and a real person on the phone when you call. Reliability is underrated in this industry — we lean into it.

Keep Learning

Septic technology evolves. We stay current on new system types, best practices, and environmental standards so you always get the right solution.

Community Matters

We live, work, and raise families in the Tennessee Valley. Protecting local groundwater and serving our neighbors isn't marketing — it's our life.
