Services

Complete Septic Care, Start to Finish

Whether it's a routine pump-out or a brand-new system, our crew handles every part of the job with the skill and respect your property deserves.

Septic Tank Pumping & Maintenance

Regular pumping is the single most important thing you can do for your septic system. We remove accumulated sludge and solids from the tank to prevent backups, odors, and premature system failure.

  • Complete removal of sludge and scum layers
  • Tank interior inspection at every pumping
  • Baffle and filter check included
  • Recommended every 3–5 years depending on usage and household size

Septic System Inspections

Buying a home, selling one, or just want peace of mind? We perform thorough inspections and provide clear, documented reports.

  • Real estate inspections for home sales and purchases
  • Routine health checks for ongoing maintenance
  • Tank level, baffle, and line assessment
  • Drain field condition evaluation
  • Written reports with photos on request

Repairs & Troubleshooting

Problems don't wait for a convenient time. We track down the root cause and fix it — no guesswork, no unnecessary excavation.

  • Broken or clogged pipes cleared and replaced
  • Tank, lid, and riser repair or replacement
  • Home backup diagnosis and resolution
  • Baffle, tee, and effluent filter replacement

Drain Field (Leach Field) Work

A failing drain field can threaten the entire system. We install, repair, and rehabilitate leach fields to keep your system healthy and your yard dry.

  • New field line installation
  • Repair or replacement of failing drain fields
  • Correction of standing water and soggy yard issues
  • Soil work and proper grading

New Septic System Installation

Building or replacing? We design and install complete septic systems for new homes and major renovations — tank, drain field, connections, and every permit in between.

  • Custom system design for your site and household size
  • Full coordination with county health departments
  • All permits handled by us
  • Tank, field lines, and service connections
  • Final inspection and walkthrough

Septic Tank Locating & Mapping

Don't know where your tank is? Neither do a lot of homeowners — especially in older houses. We locate buried tanks and map your system so future service is faster and cheaper.

  • Tank location using probing and detection tools
  • Full system layout mapping
  • Reference diagrams for homeowners
  • Prevents unnecessary digging during future service

Emergency Services — 24/7

A sewage backup is not something to wait on. Call us any time, day or night, and we'll dispatch as fast as possible.

  • Backups into toilets, tubs, or drains
  • Overflowing tanks or sewage in the yard
  • Rapid dispatch across Madison County
  • Clear communication on ETA and cost
