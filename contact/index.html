FAQ

Quick answers

How often should I pump my septic tank?

For most households, every 3–5 years. Homes with larger families, garbage disposals, or heavy water use should lean toward the shorter end. We'll give you an honest recommendation based on what we see in your tank.

Do you handle emergency backups?

Yes — 24/7. Call our main number any time and we'll dispatch as fast as possible across Madison County and surrounding areas.

Do you pull permits for new installations?

Absolutely. We coordinate all permits and inspections with the Madison County Health Department and any other applicable agencies.

Can you find my tank if I don't know where it is?

Yes. Locating buried tanks — especially on older properties — is one of our regular services. We use probing and detection tools to avoid unnecessary digging.

What areas do you serve?

Huntsville, Madison, Hampton Cove, New Hope, Owens Cross Roads, Meridianville, Hazel Green, Harvest, Gurley, Brownsboro, Toney, Monrovia, and other North Alabama communities. Not sure if we cover you? Just call and ask.
